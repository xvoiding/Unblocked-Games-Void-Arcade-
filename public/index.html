Help

Quick answers and shortcuts.

What is Void Arcade?

A clean hub with links to the big game sites, an AI assistant, free radio and a focus mixer. There are no ads on this site.

A site I opened won't load.

Big game sites are run by other companies, and many of them can't run inside another page. Use "New tab" in the top bar, or switch between Proxy and Direct.

How does the AI work?

Your messages go to an AI model through this site's server. If the site's own AI key isn't set up, it switches to a free option run by Puter, which may ask you to sign in there. Don't share personal details in chats.

Where are my scores and favorites saved?

On this device, in your browser. Clearing site data or switching devices starts you fresh.

Why is some stuff blocked?

Adult and other inappropriate content is blocked on this site.
